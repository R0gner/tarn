# Contributing

Tarn is pre-1.0 and has not been announced yet. For now:

- **Pull requests are not accepted.** The maintainer works directly on `main`.
- **Issues are welcome:** bugs, questions and ideas.

This will be revisited for 1.0. No promises about response times.

## Reporting a bug

A report that can be reproduced gets fixed sooner. Please include:

- the kit version (the file `VERSION` in the kit or in your vendored copy);
- the browser and its version, and whether it was a phone, a tablet or a desktop;
- a minimal page that shows the fault, or the gallery view (`/gallery/#/…`) in which it happens;
- what `node tools/verify.mjs` says (the last ten lines are enough), if you can run it;
- what you expected and what you saw instead; a screenshot helps.

## Ideas that help

Ideas are most useful when they start from a real board: what you are building, what the kit could not do, and what you did instead. A missing option of an
existing part or a fault in a part is easier to act on than a request for a whole new area. The kit keeps one calm, dense look; an idea that adds
decoration or a second way to do the same thing is unlikely to fit.

## Forks

The kit is under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). Forks are fine, and you are free to take it in a different direction.

## Pull requests

Pull requests opened anyway are closed with a pointer to an issue. This is not a judgement of the change; the kit has one maintainer and strict checks, and
for now the work goes through issues.
